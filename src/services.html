Services

Start with a conversation.

Every engagement starts small and earns the next step. Each step has a fixed scope and fee, agreed before we start and sized to your stage, from a founder preparing to launch to a business at €150M.

Book an Imaginary Customer Call

  1. Imaginary Customer Call

    25 minutes, no obligation
    What happens
    A conversation, not a pitch. We ask the questions, about your brand, your customers and how the company runs. No preparation needed.
    What you get
    Within 48 hours, a short note: what we heard on brand, customer and organization, where they don't line up, and 2 or 3 next steps.
    Who it suits
    Any founder or CEO who suspects the effort isn't paying off.

  2. Trifecta Audit

    The first paid step
    What happens
    For start-ups and scale-ups, a discovery with the founder. For larger companies, a discovery with the founder or CEO, plus a detailed diagnostic with the leadership team. We map how you see your brand, your customers and your business, then test it against the outside.
    What you get
    A clear read of where you stand and where you want to be, with the first ideas to test.
    How long
    Typically 1 to 4 weeks, depending on the size of the company and the number of interviews.
    Who it suits
    Founders before a funding round, a relaunch or a new market. Leadership teams whose plans keep pulling in different directions.

    Imaginary Customer Analysis. An optional starting point for companies above €1M in revenue: who your real customers are, read from your own data.

  3. Trifecta Blueprint

    Your positioning, built to last
    What happens
    Market and customer research, then the brand and the customer written as 2 portraits. The promise is where they meet.
    What you get
    Your brand manifesto, your customer manifesto, and the constitution that defines your promise and ties everything together, including the organization. Your guiding light: it stays fixed while style changes with the times.
    How long
    Typically 4 to 6 weeks.
    Who it suits
    Brands that need one clear position before they spend on design, product or media.

  4. Trifecta Strategy

    The long-term plan
    What happens
    We work backwards from where you want to be: over what horizon, what the plan is, and what it means for each area of the business. Then we go back and forth with your leadership team until every plan is locked in.
    What you get
    One plan, one forecast and one roadmap, with the blockers named up front.
    How long
    Typically 2 to 4 weeks for start-ups and smaller scale-ups, 4 to 6 weeks for larger companies.
    Who it suits
    Companies ready to commit, with a leadership team that has to own the plan.

  5. Trifecta Build

    The promise, taken to the whole company
    What happens
    The promise and the plan go to the whole company in a roadshow. Then, with the people who own each touchpoint, we map every priority customer's journey, turn the gaps into a roadmap and write the guidelines each team works from, the brand book among them. We also set who's accountable and how progress gets measured.
    What you get
    A roadmap, guidelines for every team, and a company that knows what the promise asks of it.
    How long
    Typically 9 to 24 months, depending on the size of the company.
    Who it suits
    Companies ready to put the plan to work.

  6. Trifecta Check-up

    Once a year
    What happens
    The diagnostic again, a review of the roadmap, and time to think ahead.
    What you get
    An honest answer to 2 questions: did we get where we set out to go, and is the company becoming one that keeps its promise?
    Who it suits
    Every client, every year.

How we work with you. As a project, fractional (someone senior on your team 1 to 3 days a week) or interim.
