Services

Start with a conversation.

Every engagement starts small and earns the next step. Each step has a fixed scope and fee, agreed before we start and sized to your stage, from a founder preparing to launch to a business at €150M.

Book an Imaginary Customer Call

  1. Imaginary Customer Call

    25 minutes, no obligation
    What happens
    A conversation, not a pitch. We ask the questions, about your brand, your customers and how the company runs. No preparation needed.
    What you get
    Within 48 hours, a short note: what we heard on brand, customer and organization, where they don't line up, and two or three next steps.
    Who it suits
    Any founder or CEO who suspects the effort isn't paying off.

  2. Trifecta Audit

    The first paid step
    What happens
    A discovery with the founder, or a diagnostic with your leadership team. We map how you see your brand, your customers and your business, then test it against the outside.
    What you get
    A clear read of where you stand and where you want to be, with the first ideas to test.
    Who it suits
    Founders before a funding round, a relaunch or a new market. Leadership teams whose plans keep pulling in different directions.

    Imaginary Customer Analysis. An optional starting point for companies above €1M in revenue: who your real customers are, read from your own data.

  3. Trifecta Blueprint

    Your brand strategy and positioning
    What happens
    Market and customer research, then the brand and the customer written as two portraits. The promise is where they meet.
    What you get
    Your priority customers, in order. The promise that sets you apart. A constitution that stays fixed while style changes, and guidelines for every team, the brand book among them.
    Who it suits
    Brands that need one clear position before they spend on design, product or media.

  4. Trifecta Strategy

    The long-term plan
    What happens
    We work backwards from where you want to be: over what horizon, what the plan is, and what it means for each area of the business. Then we go back and forth with your leadership team until every plan is locked in.
    What you get
    One plan, one forecast and one roadmap, with the blockers named up front.
    Who it suits
    Companies ready to commit, with a leadership team that has to own the plan.

  5. Trifecta Build

    From plan to every touchpoint
    What happens
    The promise and the plan go to the whole company. Then, with the people who own each touchpoint, we map every priority customer's journey and turn the gaps into a roadmap.
    What you get
    A business built to keep the promise, and someone senior helping run it. Delivered as a project, fractional (one to three days a week) or interim.
    Who it suits
    Founders who want the plan done, not just written.

  6. Trifecta Check-up

    Once a year
    What happens
    The diagnostic again, a review of the roadmap, and time to think ahead.
    What you get
    An honest answer to two questions: did we get where we set out to go, and is the company becoming one that keeps its promise?
    Who it suits
    Every client, every year.

How we keep it simple

Fixed scope

Every step has a scope and fee agreed before we start. When budgets are tight, we change the scope, not the quality.

The Audit counts

The Audit fee is credited in full against the Blueprint if you go ahead within 30 days.

No added budget

The work starts with what you already have: your customers, your products, your stores and your teams.
