About

Why we exist.

Too many good brands work hard for a customer who isn't there, while the ones who are slip away. Most of the time the fix isn't more budget. It's getting the brand, the customers and the company to pull the same way.

Customer centricity, in balance.

Customer centricity became the answer to everything. Put the customer first, and the rest follows. In our experience it doesn't, at least not on its own. A brand people buy because they want it, not because they need it, is a relationship with three sides: the brand, the customers and the company that serves them. Focus on one and the others drift.

MLR Consumer&Commerce exists to restore that balance for founder-led brands, start-ups and scale-ups up to €150M. We start with what you already have, because that's usually where the answer is.

We're a young company, and we say so. The experience behind the method goes back more than 20 years.

Michele Ross

Founder

Michele has spent more than 20 years in consumer brands and retail, across luxury, fashion, beauty and strategy consulting, after starting out in hospitality and retail. Her work sits where brand, customer and commercial decisions meet: who the customer really is, what the brand promises them, and whether the company is built to keep that promise.

She teaches at IED Milano and is working on a book about what most brands get wrong about customer centricity. American by birth, she lives and works in Piemonte, in English and Italian.
